How scoring works

The sheet’s first 15 variables use its existing min–max normalization across the full dataset. The final five are binary bonuses. Default weights total 1 (100%); the weighted sum is multiplied by 100. Weights are displayed rounded to two decimal places; calculations retain full precision.

Custom scores use the same values and divide by the total weight you choose, keeping the scale at 0–100. Filtering never changes the normalization or overall rank. Equal scores share a rank.
